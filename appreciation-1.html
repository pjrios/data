The seven stages

  1. Upload and confirm your assigned CSV file.
  2. Clean the imported dataset.
  3. Manually document at least three corrections.
  4. Review the locked evidence with one partner.
  5. Answer five short individual reflection questions.
  6. Compare two collection points with a two-bar display.
  7. Review and download the final evidence report.
