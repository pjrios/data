Data-cleaning notes

  • Blank or unreliable number: Use Not provided. Do not guess.
  • Negative or extremely large number: Use Not provided unless another source confirms it.
  • Repeated record: Keep the first copy and remove the later duplicate.
  • Missing unit: Add items when the table clearly measures collected items.
  • Spelling or format: Use the same correct spelling and capitalization throughout the dataset.
